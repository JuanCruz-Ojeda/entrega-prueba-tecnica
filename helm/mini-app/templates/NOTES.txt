mini-app desplegado como release "{{ .Release.Name }}" en el namespace "{{ .Release.Namespace }}".

  App:   {{ .Values.app.replicaCount }} réplica(s)  (Deployment)
  Redis: 1 réplica  (StatefulSet, PVC {{ .Values.redis.persistence.size }})

Probar la app:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "mini-app.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.app.service.port }}
  curl -fsS http://localhost:8080/health
  curl -fsS http://localhost:8080/cache-test

Correr los tests del chart:

  helm test {{ .Release.Name }}

Escalar la app a 3 réplicas:

  helm upgrade --install {{ .Release.Name }} ./helm/mini-app --set app.replicaCount=3
